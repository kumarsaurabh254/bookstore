ABOUT THE PROJECT

Made for the GL Bajaj community.

This student project is an online bookstore for GL Bajaj Institute of Technology and Management. Browse the catalogue, register, add books to your cart and try the demo order process. It was built for the Web Development lab (BCS353).

Lab experiments
